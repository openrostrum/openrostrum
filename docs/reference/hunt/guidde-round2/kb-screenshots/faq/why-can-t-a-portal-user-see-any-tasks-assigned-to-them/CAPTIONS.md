01. **Why can’t a portal user see any tasks assigned to them?**  
    `01.png` — https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/d73f1279-Screenshot-2024-01-31-at-9_05_46-AM-1.png
02. **Ensure they are logging in with the correct portal username**  
    `02.png` — https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/09dfcbb4-image-png-Feb-24-2026-10-53-48-6822-PM.png
03. **Within the Contact Profile, scroll down in the ‘Profile’ page**  
    `03.png` — https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/e67f784f-image-png-Feb-24-2026-10-55-00-0668-PM.png
04. **Locate the field ‘Portal Username’ within the Details widget on the right side of your screen**  
    `04.png` — https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/7d61beb0-image-png-Feb-24-2026-10-55-56-0939-PM.png
05. **Changing the email field will NOT change the portal username**  
    `05.png` — https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/e597261b-image-png-Feb-24-2026-10-56-42-4771-PM.png
06. **Reset portal access**  
    `06.png` — https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/0c887d47-image-png-Feb-24-2026-10-58-34-6610-PM.png
07. **Within the pop-up window, remove access from the portal user**  
    `07.png` — https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/dab8137b-image-png-Feb-24-2026-10-59-32-3444-PM.png
08. **Once portal access has been removed, you will be given the option to grant them portal access again**  
    `08.png` — https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/6214fb5e-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-00-11-7295-PM.png
09. **Once you have selected the button ‘Give Portal Access’, in the following window, click the button ‘Done’**  
    `09.png` — https://learn.sessionboard.com/images/kb/a82af21e-image-png-Feb-24-2026-11-00-42-8822-PM.png
