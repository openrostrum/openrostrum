Session participant roles

Within Sessionboard, a contact can take on different roles based on their involvement with your event. This page explains each role and how it behaves across the platform.

Note

The roles below reflect general definitions and common use cases. Every event is unique — organizers may assign or interpret these roles differently based on their own workflows.

Session submitter

Section titled “Session submitter”

The person who submits the session proposal or abstract.

  • Created on page 2 of the submission form.
  • Provides initial session details (title, description, format, etc.).
  • May or may not participate in the session as a speaker.
  • Admins can create a contact and assign them as a submitter.
  • Can receive emails from the Sessions or Contacts module, and be assigned tasks.

Tip

Consider creating a custom portal for your session submitters.

Speaker(s)

Section titled “Speaker(s)”

A person who presents during the session.

  • Delivers content, talks, or panel contributions.
  • A session can have multiple speakers — speakers are created through page 4 of the submission form.
  • Admins can create a contact and assign them as a speaker.
  • Can receive email or SMS (all speakers or select individuals) and be assigned tasks.

Chairperson

Section titled “Chairperson”

Oversees the session from a leadership or organizational perspective.

  • Ensures the session runs smoothly; may coordinate speakers or confirm participant tasks are complete.
  • Common in academic or formal conference settings; sometimes overlaps with the moderator role.
  • Only event admins can assign a chairperson.
  • Found in the Contacts module — chairpersons do not appear in the Speakers module.
  • Emails send from the Sessions module (select sessions → Send → Send Emails → choose Chairperson, Participants, or Everyone) or the Contacts module.

Moderator

Section titled “Moderator”

Facilitates the session (for example, a panel discussion).

  • Introduces speakers, keeps time, and manages audience Q&A or discussion.
  • Only event admins can assign a moderator.
  • Found in the Contacts module — moderators do not appear in the Speakers module.
  • Emails send from the Sessions module (select sessions → Send → Send Emails → choose Moderators, Participants, or Everyone) or the Contacts module.

Tip

Consider creating dedicated custom portals for your chairpersons and moderators.
